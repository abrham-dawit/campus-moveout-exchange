Moving out? Don't throw it away.

Every spring, dorms and apartments empty out and perfectly good desks, lamps, mini-fridges, and bikes end up in dumpsters. This marketplace lets students sell their stuff to other students instead — cheap for the buyer, money back for the seller, less in the landfill.

🔒 Members-only concept: the real version requires a verified school identity to post or buy, so you're dealing with actual students, not anonymous spam accounts. Listings below show a ✓ Verified student badge as part of that design.

How it works

  1. Verify once. Members confirm they're a real student (school email / ID check in the real version). That one step kills most spam and scams.
  2. Post in a minute. Photo, price, pickup area. End-of-term listings are priced to move.
  3. Message and meet on campus. Buyers contact the seller directly and meet somewhere public on campus to swap.

Honest note: this demo keeps listings in your browser only (localStorage) and the "verified" badge is a checkbox in the form — the real identity check, accounts, and messaging need a backend. See the README.
